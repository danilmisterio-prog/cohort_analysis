# Customer Cohort Analysis and Predictive Analytics

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive customer analytics workflow using Python. It combines exploratory data analysis, cohort analysis, statistical testing, customer segmentation, time series forecasting, and machine learning to understand customer behavior and generate actionable business insights.

The analysis demonstrates how transactional data can be transformed into meaningful information that supports customer retention, revenue forecasting, and data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

The project aims to answer several important business questions:

- How well are customers retained over time?
- Which customer segments generate the highest business value?
- How do discounts influence purchasing behavior?
- Are marketing activities associated with customer spending?
- Can future revenue be accurately forecasted?
- Can machine learning predict repeat purchases?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer transaction records including purchase dates, customer IDs, order values, discounts, and marketing-related information.

---

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation
- Data cleaning
- Missing value handling
- Data type conversion
- Feature engineering

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Customer behavior analysis
- Sales distribution
- Revenue trends
- Purchase patterns

### 3. Cohort Analysis
- Monthly customer cohorts
- Customer retention analysis
- Retention heatmap

### 4. Discount Analysis
- Discount effectiveness
- Relationship between discounts and purchasing behavior

### 5. Marketing Analysis
- Customer response to marketing activities
- Marketing impact on sales

### 6. Statistical Analysis
- Normality testing
- Correlation analysis
- Statistical significance testing

### 7. RFM Segmentation
- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary Value
- Customer segmentation

### 8. Revenue Forecasting
Comparison of multiple forecasting models:
- Naïve Forecast
- Moving Average
- Simple Exponential Smoothing
- Holt's Trend Method
- Holt-Winters Seasonal Model

Models were evaluated using forecasting error metrics to identify the most accurate approach.

### 9. Customer Lifetime Analysis
- First purchase analysis
- Last purchase analysis
- Customer lifespan evaluation

### 10. Machine Learning
Random Forest classifier for predicting repeat customer purchases.

# Key Insights

- Customer retention decreases progressively after the first purchase.
- High-value customer segments were successfully identified using RFM analysis.
- Discount strategies influence customer purchasing behavior but should be optimized to protect profitability.
- Statistical analysis confirmed significant relationships between multiple business variables.
- Revenue forecasting models enable more accurate business planning.
- Machine learning can successfully identify customers with a high probability of making repeat purchases.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Improve customer retention immediately after the first purchase.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customer segments.
- Optimize discount strategies using customer segmentation.
- Integrate predictive models into marketing campaigns.
- Use forecasting models to support financial and inventory planning.
- Continuously monitor customer behavior through cohort and RFM analysis.
